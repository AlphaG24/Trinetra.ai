Returning Customer Recognition

Greets existing clients by name naturally without sounding repetitive. Confirms whether previous inquiries were resolved (*"Aapki payment last time time par ho gayi thi na?"* or *"Aapki tabiyat ab kaisi hai?"*).

  • No robotic overuse: uses caller's name naturally at the start and conclusion only.
  • Direct appointment rescheduling or order lookups without asking basic details again.
  • Detects satisfaction and flags unhappy customers for priority owner attention.
Smart Scheduled Callbacks

When a customer asks for a callback at 4:30 PM, the system dials at that exact time, reminds them of the specific reason they asked for the call, and smoothly closes the booking.

  • If phone is busy or unanswered, automatically retries later rather than dropping the lead.
  • If customer declines, marks "not interested" gracefully with zero annoying spam calls.
  • Full transcript and call recording saved instantly to the customer's profile.

Customer Retention & Relationship Capabilities

Automated Contact Directory: Automatically logs caller identity, past call topics, preferences, and payment status for every interaction.
Tone & Politeness Control: Never uses abusive language, never repeats identical sentences, and maintains calm conversational poise.
1-Hour WhatsApp Reminders: Sends automated WhatsApp reminders 1 hour before scheduled appointments to eliminate no-shows.
