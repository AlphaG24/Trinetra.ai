REAL-TIME BUSINESS KNOWLEDGE SYNCHRONIZATION

Teach Your AI Simply By Speaking

Business realities shift every day. Doctors take sudden leaves, inventory gets reserved, and delivery routes change. You don't need technical staff or spreadsheets — just tell your AI what changed and it updates across all customer lines.

Routes, Loads & Prices

RETAIL & SERVICES

Stock & Festive Offers

Avoid promising out-of-stock items and apply seasonal pricing whenever you choose.

You say: "We only have Deluxe rooms left. Give 10% discount on 2-night stay."
✓ AI guides callers smoothly to available packages.

Operational Strengths of The Operations Brain

  • Updates in Real Time: Changes take effect globally across all incoming calls in under 1 second.
  • Speak or Type: Update via spoken voice notes, dashboard web forms, or WhatsApp messages.
  • Negotiation Safeguards: Preserves minimum price floors so the AI never gives away margin.
  • Zero Overlapping Bookings: Atomic calendar synchronization ensures two callers never get the same slot.
  • Complete History: Full audit ledger tracking what rules were changed and by which staff member.
  • Automated Fallbacks: Suggests alternative timings if the caller's requested time is taken.

Instant Propagation Across Channels

When an operational rule is modified, it synchronizes simultaneously across your live voice lines, WhatsApp bot, booking calendar, and billing ledger. Even during concurrent calls, callers receive the freshest pricing and accurate slot availability.
