03. Adaptive Operations

Live Business Brain & In-Call Payments

Your voice agent stays updated with today's real-world business status, and turns verbal promises into immediate bank settlements.

A. The Three Pillars of the Operations Brain
Spoken Daily Directives

Record a short voice note on your phone. The agent adopts the new schedule instantly.

"Aaj salon 10:00 baje tak band rahega to uske baad hi appointment book karna." ("The salon is closed until 10:00 AM today; please book appointments only after that.")
Live Slots & Calendar

Real-time appointment calendar sync prevents double bookings. Booked slots vanish immediately.

"11:30 baje ka slot book ho gaya hai, agla available samay 12:00 baje hai." ("The 11:30 slot is now reserved; the next opening is at 12:00 PM.")
Inventory & Pricing

Maintains available packages, transport routes, and lowest negotiation limits for accurate quotes.

"Humare paas 2 gaadiyan available hain Kanpur se Delhi route ke liye." ("We have 2 vehicles available for the Kanpur to Delhi route.")
B. 4-Step In-Call Invoice & Payment Settlement
1. Spoken Deal Extraction

Listens to the agreed price and service description during the call.

2. Statutory GST Invoice Built

Generates a clean tax invoice with your company logo, GSTIN, and tax breakup.

3. WhatsApp Invoice & QR Sent

Pushes the invoice PDF and scan-to-pay QR to caller's WhatsApp before call ends.

4. Direct Settlement

100% of customer funds transfer directly to your business account or UPI ID.
